Reel It Support

For account, booking, payment, refund, payout, privacy, safety, or technical assistance, use the in-app Help & Support area or email support@thereelit.com.

Help us locate the issue

Include your booking ID or account email and a short explanation. Never send a password, OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, full card number, banking password, or full bank-account number through email or chat.

Safety

Use the in-app SOS option during an active booking when it is safe to do so. Reel It’s SOS and support tools do not replace police, ambulance, fire, or other public emergency services. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the applicable local emergency service first.
