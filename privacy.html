Reel It Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 August 2026

Reel It is operated by Reel It Tech in Kolkata, West Bengal, India. This policy explains how Reel It handles information when customers and independent creators called Reelos use the app.

Information we collect

We may collect account identifiers, name, email, phone number, account role, selected booking or service location, booking details, messages, reviews, reports, support conversations, profile and portfolio media, device messaging/security identifiers, and payment, refund, earnings, and payout references.

Reelo profiles and payouts

A Reelo takes a camera selfie and agrees that an approved selfie becomes the public profile photo. While review is pending, the selfie is restricted to that Reelo and authorized Reel It reviewers. Authorized Reel It staff may review the profile for clarity and suitability. Reel It does not represent this as government identity verification. When a Reelo adds a bank account or UPI destination, the information is sent through secure server functions to the approved payout provider. Reel It displays masked destination information and provider references.

Location

Location is used when needed for booking addresses, service matching, ETA and safety. Reel It does not request background location permission in the current Android release and does not display a public live customer map.

Payments

Razorpay processes supported customer payment methods and RazorpayX processes eligible creator payouts. Reel It receives transaction references and statuses but should not receive or store a customer’s full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP, or banking password.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts, matching, bookings, payments, refunds, payouts, messaging, notifications, profile review, customer support, safety tools, fraud prevention, dispute handling, service improvement, accounting, and legal compliance.

Service providers and disclosure

Reel It uses service providers including Google Firebase, Razorpay and RazorpayX to operate the service. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users, investigate fraud or safety incidents, or resolve a transaction or dispute. Reel It does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Security and retention

Reel It uses authenticated access, role-based database and Storage rules, server-side payment operations, app attestation, and encrypted network connections. Information is retained only as needed for the service and for legitimate fraud, safety, dispute, accounting, tax, or legal obligations.

Your choices

You may request correction, access, or deletion of eligible information. Account deletion is available in Reel It under Settings > Delete account and through the web deletion instructions. Some financial, fraud, safety, dispute, or legally required records may be retained in restricted or anonymized form.

Children

Reel It accounts and independent bookings are intended for people aged 18 or older. A responsible adult must manage any booking involving a minor.

Contact

Email support@thereelit.com for privacy questions or requests.

This page must remain publicly accessible without signing in and must be updated whenever Reel It’s data practices change.
