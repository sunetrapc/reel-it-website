Delete your Reel It account

Reel It is operated by Reel It Tech. Customers and Reelos can request deletion in the Android app or by contacting support.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to Reel It.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Read the notice and select Request deletion.

The app signs you out and disables access while the deletion request is processed. Active bookings, refunds, disputes, or payouts must be resolved before final deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@thereelit.com from the email address registered to your Reel It account.

Use the subject “Reel It account deletion request” and state whether the account is a Customer or Reelo account. Do not email passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, full card details, or full bank-account information.

Information deleted or anonymized

Eligible account profile data, authentication access, stored profile/portfolio media, messages, notifications, support threads, and associated personal data are deleted. References in completed bookings and payout records are anonymized where the record must be retained.

Information that may be retained

Limited transaction, refund, payout, fraud, safety, dispute, accounting, tax, or legally required records may be retained for the applicable purpose and restricted from ordinary use.

Questions can be sent to support@thereelit.com.
